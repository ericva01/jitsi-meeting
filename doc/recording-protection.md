# Recording Protection

Recording Protection is a moderator-controlled conference setting that disables Jitsi Meet's built-in local recording,
Jibri/server recording, and live streaming controls and start paths. Its participant-specific moving watermark provides
visible attribution in captures made while the setting is enabled.

This is a browser application feature, not operating-system digital-rights management. It cannot block or reliably detect
OBS, Windows Game Bar, browser or operating-system capture, external cameras, audio-loopback devices, or every other form
of screen or audio capture. It does not promise black video or muted audio in external recording software.
